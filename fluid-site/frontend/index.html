// BUILT

ASCII Fluid Dynamics Simulatorjs · canvas

ASCII fluid sim (this website) based on Jos Stam’s 1999 stable-fluids method: Gauss-Seidel pressure solve, semi-Lagrangian advection, vorticity confinement to put back the curls diffusion eats. Obstacles, like this panel, are real Neumann boundaries. Semi-Lagrangian scheme took framerate up ~200% over my first naive pass.

Facial-Landmark Feedback Pipelinepython · opencv

Landmark feedback for drawings as well as photographs. Where the neural detectors gave up — rotated heads, pencil sketches — it falls back to CPD registration and solvePnP geometry. On a 50-case benchmark: 98.9% / 100% precision-recall, against GPT-5.5's 63.5% / 70.5%.
